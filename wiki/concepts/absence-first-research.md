# Absence-First Research

The organizing principle of the [[six-step-research-protocol]] in
[[schrepel-digital-brain-for-research]]: **in mature research fields, the most useful
finding is a gap.**

Content retrieval — finding what a corpus *says* about a topic — is already available
through conventional search. What is *not* available through conventional search is a
structural map of what the corpus **does not say**. A [[knowledge-graph]] produces this
map as a byproduct of extraction; querying for absent paths, disconnected components, and
undefined-but-referenced concepts turns that byproduct into a primary output.

The visible surface of this orientation is the **"What the sources do not address"**
section that the [[research-schema-design]] directs every wiki article to end with. Over
a corpus of several hundred documents, these statements accumulate into a research
agenda. "In a field structured by debates, the unanswered questions are often more useful
than the answered ones."
