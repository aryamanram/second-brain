# "Every Business Has a raw/ Directory"

A product thesis that emerged in the comments of [[karpathy-llm-knowledge-bases]]:

> "Every business has a raw/ directory. Nobody's ever compiled it. That's the product."
> — Krishna Tammireddy (Karpathy agreed: "yes exactly.")

## The idea

Every organization sits on a pile of unstructured raw material — documents, data, notes
— that no one has ever "compiled" into a navigable, queryable [[llm-knowledge-base]].
The product opportunity is to do for businesses what an LLM wiki does for personal
research: ingest the `raw/` pile and incrementally compile it into linked, summarized,
queryable knowledge.

Karpathy's own framing aligns: he sees "room here for an incredible new product instead
of a hacky collection of scripts," aimed in particular at the non-developer world that
can't vibe-code its own tooling.
