# Vibe Coding

A term **coined by [[andrej-karpathy]] in February 2025** to describe how AI tools let
people — including non-programmers and hobbyists — construct apps and websites just by
typing prompts, rather than writing code directly. (Per the Wikipedia biography,
[[wikipedia-andrej-karpathy]]; the coinage was popularized via a New York Times piece.)

## Relevance to this wiki

Vibe coding recurs throughout the knowledge-base methodology sources:

- In [[karpathy-llm-knowledge-bases]], Karpathy says he "vibe codes products with
  twitter" and that custom tools for the wiki (e.g. a small search engine over it) "are
  easy to vibe code." This positions vibe-coded CLIs as the natural way to extend an
  [[llm-knowledge-base]].
- In [[schrepel-digital-brain-for-research]], the pipeline likewise assumes that any
  custom functions a researcher needs can be vibe-coded, and that the whole system can be
  assembled by "someone comfortable with a computer" without programming expertise.

In both, vibe coding is the enabling assumption behind the claim that these systems are
"a hacky collection of scripts" away from a real product (see
[[raw-directory-as-product]]).
