# Andrej Karpathy

Slovak-Canadian AI researcher and prominent communicator (X handle **@karpathy**),
born 23 October 1986. In the X-post source he describes his personal workflow for
building [[llm-knowledge-base]]s with LLMs — the pattern this wiki is modeled on. He is
also credited as the originator of the **[[digital-brain]]** concept, which
[[thibault-schrepel]] formalizes and extends in [[schrepel-digital-brain-for-research]].

## Biography

From [[wikipedia-andrej-karpathy]]:

- **Origins & education.** Born in Bratislava; moved to Toronto at 15. BSc in Computer
  Science and Physics, [[university-of-toronto]] (2009); MSc, University of British
  Columbia (2011); **PhD, [[stanford-university]] (2015) under [[fei-fei-li]]** (NLP ×
  computer vision).
- **Career.** Founding member / research scientist, [[openai]] (2015–2017) → Director of
  AI and Autopilot Vision, [[tesla]] (2017–2022, under Elon Musk) → [[openai]] again
  (2023–2024) → founded [[eureka-labs]] (AI education, 2024) → joined **[[anthropic]]**
  pretraining team (2026).
- **Teaching.** Authored and taught Stanford's **CS 231n** (150→750 students, 2015–2017);
  "Neural Networks: Zero to Hero"; LLM101n. Earlier ran the *badmephisto* YouTube channel
  (Rubik's-cube tutorials, 9M+ views).
- **Coinages & honors.** Coined **[[vibe-coding]]** (Feb 2025); MIT TR Innovators Under 35
  (2020); Time 100 Most Influential People in AI (2024).

## Relevant positions in the knowledge-base sources

- Uses LLMs increasingly to "manipulate knowledge" (markdown + images) rather than just
  code.
- Advocates a **simple, flat** wiki: a nested directory of `.md`/`.png`/`.csv`/`.py`
  with the schema kept in `AGENTS.md`; the LLM owns and maintains all wiki content.
- Treats [[incremental-compilation]] as a human-in-the-loop process — adds sources one
  at a time, staying in the loop especially early on.
- Found that at ~small scale (~100 articles / ~400K words), auto-maintained indexes and
  summaries removed the need for fancy [[retrieval-augmented-generation]].
- Quips that he "vibe codes products with twitter" (see [[vibe-coding]]) and sees room
  for a real product in this space ("an incredible new product instead of a hacky
  collection of scripts").
