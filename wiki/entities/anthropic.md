# Anthropic

AI company; maker of the Claude family of models and of [[claude-code]], the agent
through which **this wiki is maintained**. Per [[wikipedia-andrej-karpathy]],
[[andrej-karpathy]] joined Anthropic in **May 2026** to lead a research team focused on
**pretraining**.

This is a notable connection for the wiki: the originator of the [[digital-brain]]
methodology ([[karpathy-llm-knowledge-bases]]) now works at the company whose tooling
([[claude-code]]) the methodology — and this knowledge base — runs on.
