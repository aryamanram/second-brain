# Eureka Labs

An AI education company founded by [[andrej-karpathy]] in **July 2024**, per
[[wikipedia-andrej-karpathy]]. Its first product was the AI course **LLM101n**. The
company advocates for AI teaching assistants — a concept that has drawn criticism over
data-privacy concerns and the loss of personal connection between teacher and student.

Eureka Labs sits alongside Karpathy's broader educational work, including the "Neural
Networks: Zero to Hero" series and Stanford's CS 231n.
