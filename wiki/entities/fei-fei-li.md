# Fei-Fei Li

Computer-vision researcher at [[stanford-university]] who, per
[[wikipedia-andrej-karpathy]], supervised [[andrej-karpathy]]'s **PhD (completed 2015)**
on the intersection of natural language processing and computer vision. She is a
co-author with Karpathy on the ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge paper
(2015).
