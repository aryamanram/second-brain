# Gottfried Leibniz

German mathematician and philosopher (1646–1716), one of the two independent inventors of calculus alongside [[isaac-newton|Newton]]. Like Newton, he recognized the inverse relationship between the area and tangent problems (the **Fundamental Theorem of Calculus**). Much of the standard notation of calculus derives from Leibniz.
