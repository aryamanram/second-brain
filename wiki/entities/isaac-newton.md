# Isaac Newton

English mathematician and physicist (1642–1727), one of the two independent inventors of calculus. Newton and [[gottfried-leibniz|Leibniz]] each recognized and exploited the inverse relationship between the area and tangent problems — the **Fundamental Theorem of Calculus** — turning calculus into a tool for real-world problems (see [[ch00-preview]]).
