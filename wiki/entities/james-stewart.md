# James Stewart

Mathematician and author of *Calculus*, one of the most widely used calculus textbooks. The 9th edition (Cengage, 2021) is co-authored with **Daniel Clegg** and **Saleem Watson**, who continued the work. The book is the basis for the [[stewart-calculus-9e/_hub|Stewart Calculus 9e]] pages in this wiki.
