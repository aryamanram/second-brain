# Kepano (Steph Ango)

**Steph Ango**, who goes by the handle **Kepano** (stephango.com), associated with
[[obsidian]] and its community. In [[lazy-obsidian-method-brief]], he is credited as the
creator of **[[obsidian-skills]]** (github.com/kepano/obsidian-skills) — the workflow
patterns for working inside an Obsidian vault that form the "Workflow" layer of the
[[lazy-obsidian-method]] stack.
