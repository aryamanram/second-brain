# Obsidian Skills

A set of **vault workflow patterns** by [[kepano]] (Steph Ango), hosted at
github.com/kepano/obsidian-skills. In [[lazy-obsidian-method-brief]] they form the
**"Workflow" layer** of the [[lazy-obsidian-method]] stack — practical patterns for an
agent to work effectively inside an [[obsidian]] vault.

Step 5 of the brief's 10-step setup is "Add Obsidian Skills — practical vault workflows
from Kepano," positioned after the core vault + LLM-wiki method are in place and before
adding [[graphify]] and [[qmd]].
