# OpenAI

AI research company that [[andrej-karpathy]] co-founded and worked at, per
[[wikipedia-andrej-karpathy]]. He was a founding member and research scientist from
**2015 to 2017**, specializing in deep learning and computer vision, then **returned**
from February 2023 to February 2024 before founding [[eureka-labs]].

In the wider context of this wiki, OpenAI's GPT/Codex models are noted as an alternative
backend for the [[schrepel-digital-brain-for-research]] pipeline (via [[graphify]]'s
OpenAI-compatible mode).
