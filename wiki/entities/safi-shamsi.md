# Safi Shamsi

Developer of **[[graphify]]** (github.com/safishamsi/graphify, MIT license), the
AI-assistant skill that builds the [[knowledge-graph]] at the center of the pipeline in
[[schrepel-digital-brain-for-research]]. As of April 2026 the repository had more than
27,000 GitHub stars.

In the source, [[thibault-schrepel]] credits Shamsi explicitly: the Graphify
methodology, its query commands, and the underlying graph-building (parallel extraction
agents → NetworkX graph → [[leiden-community-detection]]) are Shamsi's work, which
Schrepel adapts for research purposes rather than reimplements.
