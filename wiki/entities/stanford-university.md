# Stanford University

University where [[andrej-karpathy]] earned his **PhD (2015)** under [[fei-fei-li]], and
where he authored and taught the first deep learning course, **CS 231n: Convolutional
Neural Networks for Visual Recognition** — which grew from 150 students in 2015 to 750 in
2017 (per [[wikipedia-andrej-karpathy]]).

Stanford also appears elsewhere in this wiki via [[thibault-schrepel]]'s affiliations
(Stanford CodeX Center for Legal Informatics; Stanford Computational Antitrust Project).
