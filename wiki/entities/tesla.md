# Tesla

Electric-vehicle and AI company where [[andrej-karpathy]] served as **Director of
Artificial Intelligence and Autopilot Vision** from **2017 to 2022**, reporting to Elon
Musk, per [[wikipedia-andrej-karpathy]]. He left in July 2022 after a sabbatical, between
his two stints at [[openai]].
