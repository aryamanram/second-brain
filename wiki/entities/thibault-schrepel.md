# Thibault Schrepel

Legal scholar focused on antitrust / competition law and computational antitrust.
Affiliations given in the source: Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam (Amsterdam Law and
Technology Institute); [[stanford-university|Stanford]] CodeX Center for Legal
Informatics; [[stanford-university|Stanford]] Computational Antitrust Project.

Author of [[schrepel-digital-brain-for-research]] (forthcoming, *Network Law Review*,
Spring 2026), which adapts [[andrej-karpathy]]'s [[digital-brain]] methodology into a
research-grade pipeline built on [[graphify]] and [[markitdown]]. His stated
contribution is six research-specific adaptations (see
[[six-step-research-protocol]], [[research-schema-design]], [[hypothesis-register]],
[[graph-diagnostics]], [[claim-level-extraction]],
[[centrality-weighted-wiki-generation]]), framed as adaptations rather than original
technical work.

He applies the pipeline to two corpora: an academic research-papers corpus and a
European Commission competition-decisions corpus (508 decisions, 1977–2025), producing
the appendix research notes on computational antitrust and ecosystem analysis. Also
cited: T Schrepel, "Adaptive Regulation" (2026), *European Journal of Risk Regulation*.
