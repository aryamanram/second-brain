# University of Toronto

University where [[andrej-karpathy]] completed his bachelor's degrees in **Computer
Science and Physics (2009)**, per [[wikipedia-andrej-karpathy]]. He moved to Toronto from
Slovakia at age 15. (He subsequently earned a master's at the University of British
Columbia in 2011 and a PhD at [[stanford-university]] in 2015.)
