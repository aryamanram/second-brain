# Stewart, *Calculus* (9th Edition)

**Authors:** [[james-stewart|James Stewart]], Daniel Clegg, Saleem Watson
**Publisher:** Cengage Learning, 9th edition (2021)
**Source:** `raw/books/stewart-calculus-9e.pdf` — 1429 pages.

The standard single- and multivariable calculus textbook. Covers limits and continuity, differentiation, integration, transcendental functions, techniques and applications of integration, differential equations, parametric/polar coordinates, infinite series, and the full multivariable sequence (vectors, partial derivatives, multiple integrals, vector calculus).
