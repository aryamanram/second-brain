# Wikipedia — Andrej Karpathy

Summary of the Wikipedia biography of [[andrej-karpathy]] (clipped 2026-05-31). This is a
biographical source; it backfills the life and career detail behind the entity page,
which previously captured only his positions in [[karpathy-llm-knowledge-bases]].

## Key facts

- **Born** 23 October 1986 in Bratislava, Czechoslovakia (now Slovakia); Slovak-Canadian
  AI researcher. Moved to Toronto at age 15.
- **Education:** BSc in Computer Science and Physics, [[university-of-toronto]] (2009);
  MSc, University of British Columbia (2011, physically simulated figures under adviser
  Michiel van de Panne); **PhD, [[stanford-university]] (2015) under [[fei-fei-li]]**, on
  the intersection of NLP and computer vision.
- **Career arc:**
  - Founding member & research scientist, [[openai]] (2015–2017).
  - Director of AI and Autopilot Vision, [[tesla]] (2017–2022), reporting to Elon Musk.
  - Returned to [[openai]] (Feb 2023 – Feb 2024).
  - Founded [[eureka-labs]], an AI education company (July 2024).
  - Joined **[[anthropic]]** to lead a pretraining research team (announced May 2026).
- **Teaching & outreach:** authored and was primary instructor of Stanford's first deep
  learning course, **CS 231n** (grew from 150 students in 2015 to 750 in 2017); the
  "Neural Networks: Zero to Hero" series; the LLM101n course (Eureka Labs' first product).
  Earlier ran the *badmephisto* YouTube channel of Rubik's-cube tutorials (9M+ views).
- **Recognition:** MIT Technology Review Innovators Under 35 (2020); Time 100 Most
  Influential People in AI (2024).
- **Coined "[[vibe-coding]]"** (February 2025) — AI tools letting people build apps by
  typing prompts.

## Connection to this wiki

- Karpathy is the originator of the [[digital-brain]] / [[llm-knowledge-base]]
  methodology this wiki is built on (see [[karpathy-llm-knowledge-bases]] and
  [[schrepel-digital-brain-for-research]]).
- His coinage of [[vibe-coding]] connects to his remark in the X post that he "vibe codes
  products with twitter," and to the pipeline's reliance on vibe-coded tools.
- His 2026 move to [[anthropic]] is notable here because this wiki is maintained through
  [[claude-code]], Anthropic's agent.
